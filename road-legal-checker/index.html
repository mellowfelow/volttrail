GasGas Electric Motocross

GasGas is part of the Pierer Mobility Group — the same family as KTM and Husqvarna. The GasGas MC-E range brings the brand's distinctive Red livery to electric youth motocross, built on the same proven KTM SX-E platform. The result is a genuine gas gas electric bike with shared WP suspension, competition-spec geometry and the same READY TO RACE pedigree — just in a different colour.

The MC-E 2 suits younger riders aged 4–10, while the MC-E 5 delivers full competition performance for riders aged 12 and up. If you're searching for a gasgas ebike or gas gas e bike for your junior rider, the MC-E range is the electric motocross answer. VoltTrail stocks the GasGas MC-E electric range — contact us for current availability.

What is GasGas?

GasGas is a Spanish motorcycle brand now owned by Pierer Mobility Group (the same group as KTM and Husqvarna). In the electric off-road world, GasGas produces the MC-E 2 and MC-E 5 — youth electric motocross bikes built on the KTM SX-E platform with identical engineering in distinctive Red livery.

Is GasGas the same as KTM?

The GasGas MC-E and KTM SX-E share the same platform, motor and WP suspension — the difference is livery and minor cosmetic details. Both are built to the same competition standard. If you're deciding between a gas gas dirt bike and a KTM electric dirt bike, the choice often comes down to colour preference.

STACYC (Stability Cycle) was founded by Ryan Dungey and built in partnership with Harley-Davidson. The concept is simple: a lightweight electric balance bike that teaches young children balance, throttle control and bike handling — the core skills they need before moving to a full electric dirt bike.

The range spans from the 12eDRIVE for children aged 2–5 up to the 20eDRIVE for ages 6–10. VoltTrail stocks the full STACYC bike range in the UK — the 12eDRIVE, 16eDRIVE, 18eDRIVE and 20eDRIVE.

Zero Electric Dirt Bikes

Zero Motorcycles is a California-based electric motorcycle manufacturer with over 15 years of experience building electric two-wheelers. The Zero XB and XE are the brand's first dedicated off-road models — a new category of zero electric motorcycle built specifically for off-road use, developed under the All Access Initiative to bring electric dirt biking to a wider audience.

Both models are road-legal out of the box. The Zero XB (£3,890) is CBT-accessible — making it one of the most affordable road-legal electric motorcycles in the UK and a strong option for riders searching for a cheap electric motorcycle with real off-road capability. The Zero XE (£5,590) steps up to 21bhp and A1 licence. VoltTrail can source the Zero XB and XE — contact us for availability and lead times.

What Zero models does VoltTrail stock?

VoltTrail stocks the Zero XB and Zero XE — both road-legal electric dirt bikes. We do not currently stock other Zero models such as the Zero S, Zero SR/F, Zero FX, Zero FXE, Zero DSR or Zero DS. Those are road-oriented electric motorcycles sold through separate Zero Motorcycles dealerships.

Is the Zero XB road legal in the UK?

Yes. The Zero XB is L1e type-approved — the same licence category as a 125 electric bike. You need a CBT (Compulsory Basic Training) or a full motorcycle licence, plus insurance and an MOT after 3 years. No special licence beyond CBT is required for the XB.

What is the cheapest road-legal electric motorcycle in the UK?

The Zero XB at £3,890 is one of the most competitive road-legal electric motorcycles available in the UK with genuine off-road capability. It offers 47-mile range and CBT-accessible road use alongside trail riding.

SUR-RON

Brand Hub

Sur-Ron Electric Bikes

Sur-Ron is the UK's most popular electric dirt bike brand. Based in Chongqing, China, Sur-Ron launched the Light Bee in 2018 and changed off-road riding forever. MCIA official UK registration data confirms that Sur-Ron's Light Bee and Ultra Bee models topped the Competition category charts in 2023, recording nearly 5× more registrations than any other electric manufacturer.

VoltTrail is an authorised UK Sur-Ron dealer. All bikes come with genuine UK warranty support and access to the extensive Sur-Ron parts ecosystem.

What is a Sur-Ron?

A Sur-Ron is a Chinese-made electric off-road bike — sometimes called an electric dirt bike, e-moto or electric pit bike. The Sur-Ron Light Bee X is the world's most popular electric dirt bike, weighing around 110lb and capable of ~47mph. Sur-Rons are used for trail riding, pit-bike racing and backyard use. They are not road legal in standard form — the Sur-Ron Light Bee L1e is the road-legal version.

What is the difference between the Sur-Ron models?

The Light Bee X (LBX) is the entry-level adult model at ~110lb, 47mph. The Hyper Bee (sometimes called the mini Sur-Ron) is the youth pit-bike model for younger riders. The Ultra Bee HP is the full-size flagship at 187lb, 56mph and 55-mile range. The Storm Bee is the most powerful Sur-Ron at 265lb, 68mph and 75-mile range.

Can you upgrade a Sur-Ron battery to 72V?

Yes. The Sur-Ron LBX accepts 72V upgrade batteries from eWatt, DHS and Chi Battery Systems, delivering up to 28kW peak power. Note: 72V batteries require an aftermarket controller. VoltTrail stocks 72V upgrade batteries and compatible controllers for the LBX.

Talaria Electric Bikes

Talaria was founded by former electric motorcycle engineers who set out to improve on existing designs. The Sting range delivers a higher seat height, larger battery capacity, wider swingarm, and four levels of adjustable regenerative braking. The Komodo TL6000 targets the mid-weight performance market head-on.

Road-legal L1e variants are available for riders who want to use public roads. VoltTrail is an authorised UK Talaria dealer with stock held in the UK.

What is the difference between the Talaria Sting models?

The Talaria Sting MX3 is the entry-level 60V model. The Sting R (MX4) steps up to an upgraded 60V system with better performance. The Sting XXX (Triple X) is designed for maximum aftermarket upgrade potential. The Sting MX5 Pro (also called the Talaria Sting Pro) is the 72V flagship — the most powerful and longest-range Sting in the range.

Is the Talaria Sting road legal in the UK?

The standard Talaria Sting R, MX3, MX5 Pro and XXX are not road legal in standard configuration — they are off-road use only. The Talaria X3 (XXX) has been homologated for road use in some markets but is not currently type-approved for UK public roads. Talaria do not currently produce an L1e road-legal variant for the UK market.

Parts & Accessories

Electric Dirt Bike Batteries & Chargers

Replacement and upgrade batteries for Sur-Ron, Talaria, E Ride Pro and Revvi electric dirt bikes. Surron battery replacements, Talaria battery upgrades and quad bike battery options all in stock. OEM replacement packs restore original range and power. Upgrade batteries — 60V and 72V options from eWatt, EBMX, DHS and Chi Battery Systems — unlock significantly more range and performance from your existing bike. Free UK delivery.

72V Upgrade Batteries

A 72V battery upgrade is the single biggest performance improvement for a Sur-Ron Light Bee X or Talaria Sting. The 72V ebike battery increases peak power from ~6kW to 20–28kW depending on the pack. VoltTrail stocks eWatt, DHS and Chi 72V batteries for the LBX and Talaria platform.

60V OEM & Upgrade Batteries

The 60V battery is the standard voltage for the Sur-Ron LBX, Talaria Sting MX3 and MX4. OEM replacements restore factory spec. 60V upgrade packs from eWatt and Chi (53–70Ah) extend range by 30–50% while remaining compatible with the stock controller.

Quad Bike Batteries

Replacement batteries for kids electric quad bikes and adult ATVs. A children's quad bike battery typically runs at 12V, 24V or 36V depending on the model. Adult electric quad batteries run from 36V to 72V. Contact us to confirm the right replacement quad battery for your quad bike.

What 72V batteries fit the Sur-Ron Light Bee X?

The Sur-Ron LBX accepts several aftermarket 72V batteries including the eWatt 72V Race Battery v5 (28kW peak), the DHS GT 72V 50Ah (charger included) and the Chi Battery Gladiator X72 Compact. Note: 72V batteries require an aftermarket controller upgrade — the stock LBX controller is rated for 60V. All options are stocked at VoltTrail.

What battery does a kids quad bike use?

Most children's electric quad bikes use 12V, 24V or 36V sealed lead-acid or lithium batteries. A children's quad bike battery replacement depends on the brand and model — Revvi quads, FunBikes and Razor quads all use different specs. Contact VoltTrail with your quad model and we'll confirm the correct replacement battery.

What is the difference between an OEM battery and an upgrade battery?

An OEM (original equipment manufacturer) battery is a direct like-for-like replacement at the same voltage and capacity as the factory pack — it restores your bike to original spec. An upgrade battery increases capacity (Ah) or voltage (V) beyond the factory spec, delivering more range, more power, or both. Upgrade batteries may require a matching controller upgrade.

Legal Guide · Updated May 2025

Are Electric Dirt Bikes Legal in the UK?

Disclaimer: This guide is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Laws change — always check the latest DVLA and government guidance before riding.

The Short Answer

Yes — but with important conditions. Riding an electric dirt bike on private land with the landowner's permission is legal in the UK with no licence, insurance, or registration required. Riding on public roads requires the bike to be type-approved, registered with the DVLA, taxed, insured, and the rider must hold the appropriate licence.

Private Land Riding

Off-road use on private land is perfectly legal. This includes motocross tracks, farms, and any private land where you have permission. Most electric dirt bikes sold in the UK are designed for this use.

Road Legal Requirements

To ride on UK public roads, your electric bike must meet all of the following:

  • Factory type approval (L1e-A, L1e-B, or L3e category)
  • Registered with the DVLA and given a number plate
  • Valid MOT (if over 3 years old)
  • Road tax (currently £0 for zero-emission vehicles)
  • Valid insurance (at minimum third party)
  • Appropriate rider licence: CBT for L1e, full motorcycle licence for L3e

L1e vs L3e — What's the Difference?

L1e-A is the moped-equivalent category (max 28mph restricted, CBT required from age 16). L1e-B is a slightly faster moped class. L3e is the motorcycle equivalent, requiring a full A1 or A licence. Sur-Ron and Talaria both offer road-legal L1e variants.

ASA Warning

The ASA has upheld complaints against ads showing electric bikes being ridden on public roads without disclosing their legal classification. Always check if your bike is road-approved before using it on a public road.

Buying Guide · Updated May 2025

Best Kids Electric Dirt Bikes UK 2025

Ages 3–6: Start Safe, Start Small

For the youngest riders, look for bikes with a maximum power of 350W, a maximum speed of 8–10mph, and a parental speed limiter. The Razor MX350 (£325) and FunBikes MX350 are proven choices. Automatic throttle cutoff when the bike tips over is essential.

Ages 6–10: Building Confidence

Move up to 350W–800W in this age group. The FunBikes MXR 790W (£435) is a strong choice — lightweight, adjustable speed limiter, and long run time. Seat height adjustability is important for growing riders.

Ages 10–14: Ready for More

The FunBikes MXR 1400W (£799) and FunBikes MXR 1800W (£995) deliver proper motocross-style performance for older kids on private motocross tracks. Full suspension, hydraulic brakes, and real knobby tyres.

Age 14+: Teen / Pre-Adult

Many teens aged 14+ will be ready for a Sur-Ron Light Bee X or Talaria Sting with speed limiting enabled. These bikes can grow with the rider as they progress.
