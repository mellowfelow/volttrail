GasGas Electric Motocross

GasGas is part of the Pierer Mobility Group — the same family as KTM and Husqvarna. The MC-E range brings GasGas's distinctive Red livery to electric youth motocross, built on the same proven KTM SX-E platform with shared components, WP suspension and genuine competition-spec geometry.

The MC-E 2 suits younger riders aged 4–10, while the MC-E 5 delivers full competition performance for riders aged 12 and up. VoltTrail stocks the GasGas MC-E electric range — contact us for current availability.
