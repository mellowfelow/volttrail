Zero Electric Dirt Bikes

Zero Motorcycles is a California-based electric motorcycle manufacturer with over 15 years of experience in electric two-wheelers. The XB and XE are Zero's first dedicated off-road models, developed under the All Access Initiative to bring electric dirt biking to a wider audience at competitive price points.

Both the XB (£3,890) and XE (£5,590) are road-legal out of the box — CBT-accessible for the XB, A1 licence for the XE — making them rare dual-purpose electric dirt bikes that genuinely work on and off road.
