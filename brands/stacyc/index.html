STACYC Kids Electric Balance Bikes

STACYC (Stability Cycle) was founded by Ryan Dungey and built in partnership with Harley-Davidson. The concept is simple: a lightweight electric balance bike that teaches young children balance, throttle control and bike handling — the core skills they need before moving to a full electric dirt bike.

The range spans from the 12eDRIVE for children aged 2–5 up to the 20eDRIVE for ages 6–10. All models use standard 20V power tool batteries from DeWalt, Milwaukee and Makita — so you charge the bike the same way you charge your drill. VoltTrail stocks the STACYC range in the UK.
